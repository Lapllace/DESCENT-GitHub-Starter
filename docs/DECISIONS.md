# Decision log

Only explicitly approved decisions belong here. Proposals and experiments must be clearly labeled.

## D-001 — Threat-only scanner
- **Status:** Approved
- **Decision:** Scanner reveals relevant threats only.
- **Rules:** Safe areas remain unmarked. Collectibles, valuables and ordinary objects are not revealed.
- **Reason:** Preserve observation, discovery and meaningful risk/reward.

## D-002 — Continuous descent
- **Status:** Approved as core concept
- **Decision:** Character descends continuously; player focuses on lateral route selection and threat response.

## D-003 — Lateral traversal
- **Status:** Approved as core concept
- **Decision:** Player shifts laterally between logical columns without interrupting downward movement.
- **Open detail:** Exact column count, spacing and whether the system becomes effectively unbounded.

## D-004 — Prototype before final art
- **Status:** Approved
- **Decision:** Validate movement, scanner, procedural hazards and route fairness with graybox geometry first.

## D-005 — Team source of truth
- **Status:** Approved
- **Decision:** GitHub is the official record for approved code, documentation and decisions. AI outputs are proposals until reviewed by a human.
